Setup

From bulb to menu bar in three steps.

YeelightBar discovers compatible lights on your local network, then waits for you to decide which devices to trust.

  1. Enable LAN Control

    Open the official Yeelight mobile app and enable LAN Control for each compatible light.

  2. Join the same trusted network

    Keep your Mac and light on the same local network, then allow Local Network access when macOS asks.

  3. Discover, review, and add

    Open YeelightBar, run discovery, review the device, and explicitly add it. Nothing is trusted automatically.

LAN

Direct means direct.

Your controls travel from your Mac to your light over the local network. Manual IP connection is available when discovery is not.

Privacy & security

Your light should not need another cloud.

YeelightBar keeps device information and preferences in its macOS sandbox and does not operate a server that receives your activity.

No account.
No analytics.
No telemetry.

The app connects only to lights you approve. Discovery candidates are never trusted automatically, and endpoint changes require confirmation.

Light controls
Travel directly over your private or local network to the selected light.
Preferences
Stay in the app sandbox unless you explicitly export them to a location you choose.
Update checks
Contact GitHub over HTTPS only when you choose Check for Updates. Automatic background checks are off.

Protocol limitation: the upstream Yeelight LAN protocol is plaintext and unauthenticated. Use YeelightBar only on a network you trust.

Compatibility

Built for Yeelight LAN Control.

Compatibility depends on local protocol support, not the brand name alone. Xiaomi or Mijia devices work only when their firmware exposes Yeelight LAN Control.

Mac

  • macOS Ventura 13 or later
  • Apple Silicon and Intel
  • Local Network permission

Lights

  • Wi-Fi bulbs and light strips
  • Ceiling, desk, and ambient lights
  • Yeelight LAN Control enabled

Not supported

  • Cloud-only devices
  • BLE Mesh and Zigbee lights
  • Matter or HomeKit-only lights
